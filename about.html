About Gold Pelet

Your process-line partner in pellet snacks.

Founded in 1998, Gold Pelet builds long-term supply relationships with food brands and co-packers — not one-off orders.

Our Story

Built on one idea: a pellet supplier you don't have to babysit.

Gold Pelet was founded in 1998 to solve a problem the founders had lived through as snack producers themselves: inconsistent pellet quality that forced constant fryer recalibration. We built our first extrusion line around fixed moisture and density tolerances — and every line since has held to that same standard.

Today, four production lines run from a single facility, supplying potato, wheat, corn and micro pellets to food brands and co-packers in 25+ countries.

Milestones

15+ years, four expansions.

1998

Founded, first extrusion line installed

Gold Pelet opens with a single potato-pellet line, supplying local snack producers.

2006

Wheat and corn lines added

Facility expands to three parallel lines, and the export program begins with its first regional shipments.

2014

ISO 22000 and ISO 9001 certified

Full facility audit and certification program completed, opening the door to major international accounts.

2021

Fourth line and micro-pellet capability

A fourth extrusion line comes online, adding sub-5mm micro pellets and custom grain blends to the catalog.

Today

Exporting to 25+ countries

50+ tonnes/day peak capacity, supplying food brands and co-packers across five continents.
