# Product category image credits

`wheat-raw.jpg`, `wheat-fried.jpg`, `corn-raw.jpg`, `corn-fried.jpg`,
`potato-raw.jpg` and `potato-fried.jpg` are real Gold Pelet product
photography — close-up crops taken from the official raw/fried split-bowl
photos published on goldpeletcips.com/foto-galeri, composited onto a
category-consistent solid background (see `assets/img/products/` for the
full source photos and `gp-*` originals). These replace the earlier stock
stand-in photography (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY / CC BY-SA) that occupied
these filenames before real product photography was available.
